DeepSeek Research 2.0

A research department operating alongside the broader DeepSeek model line. Public access is limited to benchmark records and the direct chat interface. Whitepapers and open research remain request-only.

What's public

Benchmarks. Strata Depth Index runs and model speed records are maintained as the public scorecard.

Chat interface. Direct access to the research chat is available without registration. The chat stays separated from the public showcase.

What's request-only

Whitepapers. Internal whitepapers are not described publicly. Access is handled through the request flow.

Open Research archive. Civilization, foundational, technical, medical, and security research transferred from prior public repositories. The archive index is restricted; section descriptions are public.
