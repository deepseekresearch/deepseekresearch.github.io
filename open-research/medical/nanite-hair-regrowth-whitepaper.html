ABSTRACT

The Oroboros Nanite Hair Regrowth Unit is a biocompatible nano-mesh patch that reconstructs hair follicles in regions where follicles no longer exist — full baldness, burn scars, advanced alopecia, and traction damage. The patch delivers DNA-locked surgical nanites and autologous dermal-papilla primers into the target region and dissolves within 72 hours, leaving behind live, cycling follicles.

Unlike frequency-based devices that reactivate dormant follicles, this unit builds follicles from the ground up. It is indicated when residual follicular structure is absent or insufficient.

Part II

2.0 Mechanism

2.1 The Mesh

A single-use polysaccharide mesh, 5 mm thick, conforms to the scalp's curvature and adheres by pressure. The mesh is breathable, shower-safe after 12 hours, and absorbs fully by the 72-hour mark.

2.2 The Payload

Embedded within the mesh are two payloads: surgical nanites (~106 per cm²) fused to the patient's NOIR Key, and autologous dermal papilla primers banked at patient onboarding. The nanites descend through the stratum corneum in the first 30 minutes and begin follicle reconstruction in the upper dermis.

2.3 Reconstruction Sequence

  • Hour 0–1: Nanites anchor in the dermis. The NOIR Key handshake verifies host identity.
  • Hour 1–6: Dermal papilla seeds differentiate. Matrix cells begin proliferating around each new papilla.
  • Hour 6–24: Hair bulb forms. Vascular tethering to local capillaries is established. Sebaceous gland is initialized.
  • Hour 24–72: Outer root sheath forms. First shaft keratinization begins. Mesh dissolves.
  • Day 3–5: New shafts emerge through the epidermis. Normal anagen cycle takes over.

7.0 Safety and Contraindications

Contraindications

  • Active scalp infection
  • Open wounds at the target site
  • Active chemotherapy (use between cycles)
  • Polysaccharide mesh allergy (verify with included 24-hour skin test)

Expected Effects

  • Mild warmth under the mesh during activation
  • Light itching at day 3–5 (shaft emergence)
  • Pink flush post-removal for 2–3 days
  • No systemic exposure — nanites are DNA-locked and dermal-confined
