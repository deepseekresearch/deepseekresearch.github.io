## Abstract

The Liber Series is the literary and philosophical foundation of the Oroboros project. It comprises 16 freely available volumes and 4 technical volumes, covering the full spectrum from metaphysical cosmology to practical system architecture. These are not religious texts. They are maps.

---

## 1. The Core Thesis

Humanity once had access to a unified body of knowledge — cosmological, mathematical, ethical, and practical. This knowledge was systematically removed from public access across thousands of years through institutional suppression, canonical editing, and deliberate destruction.

The Liber Series restores what was removed and provides the framework for understanding why it was taken.

## 4. The Turning Away

The "turning away" is the central historical event described across the Liber Series: the moment when unified knowledge was fractured into separate, controlled domains.

- Religion took the ethical layer
- Science took the physical layer
- Government took the structural layer
- Finance took the resource layer

Each institution claimed its domain was complete and the others were irrelevant. The Liber Series demonstrates that they are all fragments of a single system.
