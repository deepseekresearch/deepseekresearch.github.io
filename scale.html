IV. Scaling Methodology

The expansion between lattice dimensions follows three documented mechanisms. Each preserves φ/√2 ratio integrity while exponentially increasing computational capacity.

○ Mirror Expansion

Primary scaling mechanism. A lattice at n³ reflects to (2n)³, then +1 breaks symmetry. Produces 8.25× state expansion while preserving all architectural properties.

Example: 48³ → 96³ + 1 = 97³ · Ratio: 8.2526×

φ φ-Harmonic Step

Golden ratio stepping between non-mirror dimensions. Each φ-step increases dimension by factor of 1.618, with corresponding state expansion of φ³ ≈ 4.236×.

Example: 97³ → 157³ (φ-step) · Ratio: φ³ ≈ 4.236×

▦ Pod Replication

Horizontal scaling through identical pod duplication. Each new pod adds full surface states while sharing the neural cube substrate across all pods.

Example: 1 pod → 97 pods (Kaiju 97³) · Linear state multiplication
